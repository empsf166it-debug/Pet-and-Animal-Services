About GroomPaws

Grooming is self-care for the ones who can't ask for it.

Founded by a team of certified stylists and animal lovers, GroomPaws blends salon-level artistry with a slow, calm, fear-free approach. No rushing, no cages, no harsh chemicals — just patient hands and a lot of treats.

Regular professional grooming isn't just about looks. It prevents painful matting, catches skin issues early, keeps nails and ears healthy and helps your pet feel genuinely comfortable in their own coat.

Pet Preparation & Care Guide

A little prep makes a happier groom

Our groomers' favourite tips for before, during and after your visit.

01

Before the Groom

  • • Take a relaxing walk & potty break first.
  • • Skip big meals 2 hrs before.
  • • Tell us about allergies or sore spots.
02

Bathing & Brushing Tips

  • • Brush long coats 3–4× weekly to prevent mats.
  • • Use pet-safe, pH-balanced shampoo only.
  • • Bathe every 4–6 weeks for most breeds.
03

What to Bring

  • • Vaccination records (rabies, DHPP / FVRCP).
  • • Leash or secure carrier.
  • • A favourite toy or blanket for comfort.
04

Puppy & Kitten Prep

Handle paws, ears and mouth daily at home and play hair-dryer sounds softly so the salon feels familiar.

05

Post-Grooming Care

  • • Watch for redness after close clips.
  • • Avoid swimming or baths for 48 hrs.
  • • Keep brushing to extend the fresh look.
06

Calming Nervous Pets

  • • Book quiet morning slots.
  • • Keep goodbyes short and cheerful.
  • • Ask for our Fear-Free, low-noise grooming.
